Time Management

Time is one of the most vslusble things in life. Unlike money or material possessions, time cannot be saved for future use once it has been wasted. Successful people understand the value of time and use it wisely. They make plans, set goals and avoid spending too much time on activities that do not benefit them.

Students, in particulr, need to manage their time properly. A student who creates a good timetable can balance schoolwork, household responsibilities, rest and recreation. Studying regurlarly also makes it easier to understand lessons and prepare for examinations.
